Frequently Asked Questions

How does an AI mock interview work on InterviewAce?

InterviewAce simulates a real hiring manager interview using Google Gemini 1.5 Flash. You converse via real-time voice or interactive chat. The AI asks technical and behavioral questions, evaluates your answers, and provides an immediate score breakdown.

Can InterviewAce generate interview questions from my resume and job description?

Yes. Uploading your PDF resume along with your target job description allows InterviewAce to extract your skill stack, identify ATS gaps, and generate customized role-specific questions.

Is InterviewAce free to use for technical interview practice?

Yes, InterviewAce offers a free tier allowing software engineers to practice mock interviews, generate assessment questions, and analyze their resumes without credit card requirements.

How realistic is the AI voice interview simulation?

InterviewAce uses multimodal Gemini audio transcription and low-latency Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech synthesis (with native browser Web Speech API fallback) to deliver natural verbal interview exchanges.

What tech stacks and developer roles are supported?

Frontend (React, Vue, TypeScript, Next.js), Backend (Node.js, Express, Python, Java, Go), Full Stack, DevOps, System Design, and Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA).

How does AI scoring compare to a human mock interviewer?

InterviewAce evaluates answers across four objective vectors: Technical Accuracy, Depth & Edge Cases, Communication Clarity, and Structure (e.g. STAR method), delivering itemized rubrics with zero scheduling delay.
